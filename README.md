> My boss booked a meeting in the focus time I blocked on my calendar, titled 'quick sync about focus time'.

The joke turns on a small, universally recognised office contradiction: blocking focus time on a shared calendar is supposed to protect your attention, yet the calendar itself is the tool a manager uses to override it. The punch lands in the meeting's title, which cheerfully names the very thing it destroys — a "quick sync about focus time" is, by definition, not focus time. Everyone who has watched a carefully guarded block of quiet work vanish into someone else's invite nods first, then laughs at the absurdity of the label.
